How it works

WatchHistory saves YouTube progress and history in your browser. It does not use an account, cloud sync, or its own server.

Your data

  • Video history and watch data are stored in IndexedDB.
  • Settings are stored in your browser's local extension storage.
  • While you watch YouTube, the extension saves progress so unfinished videos can resume later.

When official YouTube watch history is disabled

When official YouTube watch history is off, YouTube's Home recommendations and History page become much less useful. We recommend enabling these options in Options:

You can review or change your official YouTube watch history setting in Google My Activity.

  • Redirect YouTube history: opens your local WatchHistory page instead of youtube.com/feed/history.
  • Redirect Home & Shorts to Subscriptions: skips recommendations and sends the YouTube Home page and Shorts feed to your Subscriptions feed.

Open Options

YouTube site access

WatchHistory needs access to https://www.youtube.com to save progress and show features such as resume badges and the Continue Watching shelf.

  • In Firefox, open Manage Extension from the extension menu (or open about:addons) and enable Access your data for https://www.youtube.com.
  • In Chrome or Edge, open Manage extension, then enable YouTube under Site access.
  • Reload YouTube after changing the permission.

Back up your history

Your history is only stored locally. It can be lost if you clear browser data, reset or reinstall your browser, remove the extension, or lose the device.

  • Open Options and use Export to save a JSON backup.
  • Keep backups in more than one place, such as your computer and a separate drive or cloud storage service.
  • Use Import in Options to restore an exported backup.
