See It In Action

Your history stays in this browser profile. The extension doesn’t upload it to us, and there’s no automatic cloud sync. Export a backup before removing the extension or clearing its stored data.

Local storage

Saved history and settings live in your browser profile. Storage is subject to browser and device limits.

No analytics

You control your records

Delete saved videos, clear your history, or export a JSON backup from options.

Before you install

Can it recover my old YouTube history?

No. The extension records activity going forward. It cannot recover deleted official history or activity from before installation.

Does it work while I’m signed out?

Yes. The extension saves its own history in your browser, so you can find videos and resume them while signed out or with YouTube history disabled.

How do backups work?

Export a JSON backup from options and keep it somewhere safe. Importing JSON replaces your existing saved history. Export first if you want to keep the current records.

What does Ghost Mode do?

It pauses the extension’s history, progress, and watch-time recording for the session. It does not make YouTube browsing anonymous.

Why I built WatchHistory for YouTube™

I prefer using YouTube with watch history turned off, but I still wanted to find videos I’d watched and pick up where I stopped.

I built it to keep those useful features without turning YouTube history back on. It saves history and playback progress locally in your browser, with no analytics or cloud sync.

It’s an independent, open-source project built around that simple idea: keeping a useful history in your browser instead of relying on your YouTube account.
